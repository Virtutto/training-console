# Calibration battery - field sheet

Fill this in at the bar. One max attempt per test (test 1 gets two). Stop at form
break, not pain. Full design + the mapping rules live in:
`~/.config/opencode/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-training-console-calibration-design.md`

Do not run this within 24h of a hard session, and not on a bouldering day.

## Warm-up first (10 min, not optional)

Wrist circles + prayer stretch + floor rocking, shoulder CARs, 5 easy scapular
pull-ups, 2 easy tuck lever entries, 2 wall plank holds, leg swings, 10 squats.
